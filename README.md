### 👋 About Me

I like systems that are **observable**, networks that are **traceable**,
and algorithms that are **well-defined and explainable**.

I'm an MEng Computer Engineering student at the **University of Ioannina**, based in **Thessaloniki, Greece**.
I enjoy working close to the fundamentals: how data moves through memory,
how packets move through the network, and how algorithms shape performance.

- 🖥️ **IT Systems Administrator Intern @ Masoutis S.A.** — rolled out **Zabbix monitoring across 22,000+ sensors**, with SNMP traps and triggers, and configured VLANs on Cisco RV and HPE Aruba CX 6000 switches.
- 🛠️ **IT Support Technician @ University of Ioannina (CSE Dept.)** — kept the department's systems running for 2+ years and built tools for students and staff.
- 🔐 Currently building **security and systems tooling** for Linux servers.
- 🗣️ Greek · English · French
